Privacy Policy

Updated: 9 November 2026

In short

Binenu does not collect, store or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The names you type at the start of a game and your sound preference are saved only on your device, so you do not have to type them again. You can remove them at any time by clearing the names or deleting the app.

Card content and your answers are never saved or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Decks are bought through Apple. We never see or store payment details. Apple tells the app which decks belong to your Apple Account.

Recording a game

If you choose to record a game, the app records its own screen, the front camera and the microphone, only from the moment you start until you stop. A red badge with the recording time is shown the whole time.

The video is kept only inside the app on your device and is excluded from backups. It leaves the device only if you save it to Photos or share it yourself. You can delete any video in the editor or in Settings.

Permissions

Camera, microphone and saving to Photos are used only for recording a game, and only when you choose to. For Photos the app asks for add only access and never sees your photos. The app does not ask for location or contacts.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because there is no such data. The app has no ads.

Children

The game is meant for adult couples. The Spicy deck contains mature content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page and in the app, with the date of the change.
