How to play

Pick a deck, add both names if you like, and tear the pack open. Each turn reveals a card: a truth is a question you answer honestly, a dare is a small task. Then the turn passes.

Restore purchases

Bought a deck and do not see it? Open Settings (the gear on the main screen) and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy
